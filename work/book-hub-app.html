01

Overview

I was part of a team that worked on a book application. We set out to solve the challenges readers face: slow book delivery, limited Arabic content, high prices and few payment options — plus missing features like book borrowing and cross-device sync. Similar apps were often complicated and not user-friendly.

Applying the stages of design thinking, we created a book app that is simple to use, suited to readers' real needs, and balanced between attractive design and practical function.

02

Challenge & solution

The problem

  • Slow delivery of physical books
  • Limited Arabic content
  • High prices and restricted payment options
  • No option to borrow books
  • Reading features missing: sync, offline, notes, night mode

Our solution

  • Faster delivery through local partners and warehouses
  • A rich Arabic library via local publishers
  • Multiple payment options: card, wallets, cash
  • Borrow books by the day at a lower cost
  • Sync, offline reading, highlights, notes and night mode

Persona

OH

Omar Hassan

21 · CairoSingleUniversity student

Omar relies heavily on e-books for his studies because he dislikes carrying heavy paper books. He reads on his phone during commutes and breaks, prefers fast, easy platforms in Arabic, and is influenced by recommendations from classmates and social media.

Goals
  • Fast access to study books
  • Arabic content that's easy to find
  • Affordable reading options
Frustrations
  • Physical book delivery takes too long
  • Limited Arabic academic content
  • Apps that are slow or complicated

07

Key features

Buy or borrow

Every book can be bought or borrowed per day, with the total shown before you pay.

My booklist

Track New, Reading and Finished books, and know when a borrowed book is ready for pickup.

Comfortable reader

Adjust font size, line spacing and margins, with bookmarks and search inside the book.

Reading plans

6- and 12-month subscriptions with unlimited downloads for offline reading.

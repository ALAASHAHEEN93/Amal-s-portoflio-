Overview

The Book Hub landing page is the web front door for the Book Hub reading app. It had one job: make a first-time visitor feel the joy of reading in a few seconds — then send them to browse books or download the app.

It was designed alongside the Book Hub mobile app, so it shares the same brand, typography and book-first visuals, and speaks to the same readers: students and book lovers looking for affordable books and more Arabic content.

02

Challenge & solution

The problem

  • Visitors don't know what Book Hub offers at first glance
  • Long pages hide the books behind text
  • No clear path from the website to the app
  • Hard to browse by interest before signing up

Our solution

  • A hero that leads with an author spotlight and a reading quote
  • Books and covers above the fold — not paragraphs
  • A clear “Download our app” section
  • Category chips and a best-seller shelf to explore instantly

08

Design decisions

01Author-first hero. Leading with a celebrated author makes the page feel like a bookstore, not a tech product.
02One idea per section. Each block — quote, app, categories, best sellers — answers one question a visitor has.
03Free shipping up front. A small badge in the hero removes a common reason people hesitate to buy books online.

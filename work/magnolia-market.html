Overview

A concept shopping app for Magnolia Market, the home goods and lifestyle brand. The aim: your perfect choice for a modern home — a personalized experience, unique pieces you may not find in the local market, other shoppers' reviews and order tracking.

The challenge was to carry the brand's warm, farmhouse-modern feeling into a mobile store that is still fast to browse and simple to buy from.

02

Challenge & solution

The problem

  • Unique pieces are hard to discover in a long catalogue
  • Hard to imagine how décor will look at home
  • Shoppers rely on reviews but can't find them
  • Checkout feels long on mobile

Our solution

  • Curated collections with sort and filter
  • AR placement with coach marks to try a piece in your room
  • Ratings shown on every product card
  • A short cart → PayPal checkout

Design decisions

01Soft pink + olive palette. #F8E3E0 and #3F5E29 echo the brand's natural, homely mood.
02Poppins for clarity. A friendly geometric typeface keeps prices and names easy to scan.
03Coach marks for AR. Step-by-step hints (detect surface → tap to place) make a new feature easy to try.

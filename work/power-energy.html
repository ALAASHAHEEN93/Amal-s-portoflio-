Overview

Power Energy is an e-commerce website for electrical and electronic products — cameras, telephones and gaming consoles. The goal was a store that feels trustworthy and makes it easy to compare products and buy.

I worked from wireframes to a full visual design, with a bold berry colour palette that gives the store a distinctive, premium look.

02

Challenge & solution

The problem

  • Electronics stores feel cluttered and hard to scan
  • Shoppers can't compare prices quickly
  • Little trust in unknown online stores

Our solution

  • A clean home page that leads with popular products
  • Category pages with price and popularity filters
  • Customer feedback and an About page that tells the brand story

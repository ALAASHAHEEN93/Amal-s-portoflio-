Weather App

A clean weather app UI with hourly and 7-day forecasts, air quality, UV index and sunrise times.

01

Overview

A weather app UI focused on at-a-glance information. Most people open a weather app for a few seconds, so every screen answers one question fast: What's it like now? What about later today? And this week?

The visual direction uses a deep purple night sky, soft 3D weather icons and glassy cards, so the information feels calm and easy to read.

02

Challenge & solution

The problem

  • Weather apps cram too much data on one screen
  • Hourly and weekly forecasts are hard to compare
  • Extra info (air quality, UV) is buried in menus

Our solution

  • One focus per screen: now, today, this week
  • Swipeable hourly and 7-day rows
  • Air quality, sunrise and UV as clear cards

Design decisions

013D icons. Soft, illustrated weather icons make conditions instantly recognizable.
02Glass cards. Translucent cards keep the background mood while separating information.
03Yellow call-to-action. A warm yellow “Get Start” button stands out against the purple sky.

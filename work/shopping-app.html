Overview

A shopping app for finding the best gifts for your loved ones — focused on watches and accessories. The idea: make gift shopping feel special, from browsing new arrivals to checking out with a single swipe.

Clean outlined cards on a warm cream background let the product photography do the talking.

02

Challenge & solution

The problem

  • Gift shopping takes too long
  • Too many products, little guidance
  • Checkout feels like a chore

Our solution

  • New arrivals and “We recommend” sections
  • Simple categories: watches, wrist bands, wonders
  • Swipe to pay — one gesture to finish

Design decisions

01Outlined cards. Thin outlines keep the UI light so product photos stand out.
02Deep green accent. Matches the luxury gift boxes in the photography.
03Swipe instead of tap. Prevents accidental purchases and feels satisfying.

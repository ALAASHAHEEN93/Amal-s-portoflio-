01

Overview

A coffee ordering app covering the whole journey: sign in, browse the hot and iced menu, customise size, add to cart, pay by card and track the delivery live.

The design uses warm coffee browns and simple layouts so ordering a drink takes only a few taps.

02

Challenge & solution

The problem

  • Ordering coffee by phone or in a queue is slow
  • Menus don't show prices and sizes clearly
  • No way to know when the order will arrive

Our solution

  • A visual menu with categories, prices and ratings
  • Size selection on the product page
  • Live tracking with courier name and ETA

Design decisions

01Coffee brown palette. #B46E41 sets a warm, café-like mood.
02Two prices on cards. Showing the original and the deal price highlights offers at a glance.
03Map tracking. Seeing the courier and the minutes left reduces “where is my order?” anxiety.
